Please be advised that RF transmissions from OTA POWDER radios have the potential to disrupt third-party (production) wireless systems in the area around the University of Utah and into Salt Lake City. While POWDER takes measures to monitor and alert on out-of-compliance transmissions, IT IS YOUR RESPONSIBILITY TO ONLY TRANSMIT WHERE PERMITTED BY FCC REGULATIONS. Further, you are required to abide by ALL FCC regulations, including those governing reception and decoding of third-party signals. In general, you may not operate in licensed bands where neither you nor the POWDER platform have a valid, active license. POWDER requires that you declare the spectrum in which you intend to transmit in any experiment run on the platform. (This is done with specific syntax in the associated profile script. Consult the documentation and/or example profiles provided by the POWDER team for details. Currently available spectrum is listed on the POWDER Resource Availability web page on the website.)
