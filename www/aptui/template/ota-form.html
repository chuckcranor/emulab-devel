POWDER Over-The-Air (OTA) Use Agreement

As the leader of the project on POWDER, you are responsible for reading and agreeing to the conditions presented in this document before use of OTA resources on POWDER will be granted. Please share the text of this agreement with all project members.

As part of the process to enable access to POWDER OTA resources, please fill in the form below to provide details on how your project plans to operate over-the-air.

This information will be kept confidential, and is meant to inform POWDER operators of the types of transmissions to anticipate. If your project's OTA operation plan changes, you must send an updated write-up to the powder-support@powderwireless.net BEFORE operating according to your updated scheme. We will review your submitted information and either grant your OTA access request, or follow up with you if more discussion or information is required. This request may take a few days to process.

Note: Access to certain resources, such as Massive MIMO devices, requires additional review and permissions.
